```text
The 24 positive-definite even unimodular lattices in dimension 24, classified by Niemeier in 1973, play a foundational role at the intersection of sporadic finite group theory, algebraic geometry, and string theory. In this paper, we present the complete, machine-certified formal specification and interactive verification in Lean 4 of the Niemeier lattice N(A_8^3) with root system A_8 + A_8 + A_8. We construct the root sublattice R in R^24 of rank 24, Coxeter number h = 9, 216 roots of squared length 2, and determinant det(R) = 9^3 = 729. We formally prove that the ternary glue code G_glue in (A_8* / A_8)^3 isomorphic to (Z/9Z)^3 of order |G_glue| = 27 = 3^3, generated by the isotropic vector g = (1, 1, 4), exhibits exact even integer norm squared ||g||^2 = 8/9 + 8/9 + 20/9 = 4 in 2Z, guaranteeing that the extended lattice N(A_8^3) is strictly even and unimodular with det(N) = 729 / 27^2 = 1.0000. Furthermore, we establish the bridge to the differential topology of complex K3 surfaces, formally linking the lattice rank to the topological Euler characteristic chi(K3) = 24 and Hodge numbers (h^{2,0}, h^{1,1}, h^{0,2}) = (1, 20, 1), and formalize the Umbral Moonshine connection at lambency ell = h = 9 with mock modular forms of weight 1/2 and shadow weight 3/2. The entire formalization is certified in Lean 4 without custom axioms or unproven conjectures (0 sorry), supported by an exact algorithmic verification oracle, and deposited permanently in the CERN Zenodo open science repository (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23324067).
```
